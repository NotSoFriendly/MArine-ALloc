Ocean allocation planner

Paint fish stock on the grid, add fleets with an urgency and a fishing area, then press Go. Pressing Go sends everything to your C++ engine (via server.py) and shows what it returns.

1. Ocean

Click or drag over cells to set their fish stock. Use 0 for open water. Touching cells form one shoal.

2. Fleets

Area: none yet. Switch to “Pick fishing area” and drag on the grid.
